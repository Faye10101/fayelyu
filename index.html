About

A designer who codes
and cares about the why

I'm Faye Lyu, a second-year Bachelor of Design student majoring in User Experience Design at the University of Melbourne. I came to UX from a genuine curiosity about how people think and behave — not just what they do, but why.

My work tends to live at the edge of research and craft. I care deeply about the moments of friction that go unnoticed — the unclear navigation, the broken purchase flow, the teammate who gets ignored. Good design, to me, is noticing those moments and doing something quiet and useful about them.
